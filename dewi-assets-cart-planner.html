Summary

Plan before teachers start naming.

Right now, every cart in Dewi is named by the teacher who scans it first — which can lead to inconsistencies like "Cart 1", "cart1", "CART 01", or simply no name at all. This wizard lets you set the convention before any teacher touches a cart.

The plan you create here is a reference document — it does not automatically name carts in Dewi. Teachers type the exact same name into Dewi when they scan devices, matching your plan.

1
Pick a naming style Numeric (Cart 1, Cart 2…) or alphabetic (Cart A, Cart B…). This choice is locked in for your school once you add the first cart.
2
Add each cart For each cart: the room it lives in, and any sharing arrangement (e.g. "shared with 6-5 Murphy"). Labels are auto-generated in sequence — no free typing.
3
